How this bot trades

  1. ICHI (Ichimoku 10/30/60): Tenkan crosses above Kijun with price closing above the cloud. Stop 2.5×ATR(14). Exit on a 4H close back below Kijun.
  2. GEM (refined EMA): EMA20 crosses above EMA50, or price reclaims EMA20 while EMA20>EMA50. Stop 2×ATR(14). Exit when EMA20 crosses back below EMA50.
  3. Both engines only fire while BTC's own 4H trend is up (panel above), and skip any coin whose ATR(14) is over 4% of price -- too noisy for the fixed stop distance to mean anything.
  4. Position sizing: ICHI risks 0.5% of paper equity per trade, GEM risks 0.25%; max 5 open positions per engine; after 8 straight losses in an engine, it trades at half risk until its next win.

Paper trading only. This page shows the same signal logic the bot runs on GitHub, computed live in your browser -- it is not a buy/sell recommendation by itself.
